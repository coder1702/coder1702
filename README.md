> **MediQueue aims to reduce patient waiting times while reducing the workload of hospital reception staff.**

<br>

<table>
<tr>

<td width="50%" valign="top">

### 🎯 The Problem

Traditional hospital visits often involve:

- 🕐 Long waiting times
- 🧑‍💼 Reception workload
- 📋 Manual appointment handling
- 📞 Difficulty managing queues
- 📄 Scattered medical information

</td>

<td width="50%" valign="top">

### 💡 The Solution

MediQueue brings the process into one platform:

- 🎫 Digital token booking
- 🏥 Hospital discovery
- 👨‍⚕️ Doctor management
- 📅 Appointment tracking
- 📊 Medical history
- 🤖 AI-powered assistance

</td>

</tr>
</table>
